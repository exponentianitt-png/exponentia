About Us

National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (NITT) is one of India’s premier institutions, known for its academic excellence, strong research culture and vibrant campus life. NITT stands as a symbol of academic excellence, innovation and integrity. NITT has secured a prestigious position in the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF), being ranked among the top 10 engineering colleges in the country and the #1 NIT overall, reflecting its excellence in academics, research and innovation. In subject specific metrics such as Mathematics, the institute also shines with a strong national rank. With a legacy of producing outstanding engineers and scientists. Rooted in tradition yet driven by modern vision, NITT continues to shape minds that contribute meaningfully to science, technology and society.

The Department of Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli is one of the institute’s most distinguished academic units. With a multidisciplinary approach to teaching and research, the department focuses on providing mathematical solutions to socio-economic, technological and real-world challenges. Supported by eighteen faculty members across major areas of mathematics, the department actively contributes to advanced research, quality education, international collaborations, and the organization of conferences, workshops and outreach programs.

Exponentia is the annual flagship event of the Department of Mathematics, organized to celebrate mathematics beyond the classroom. It provides a platform for students to explore ideas, showcase talents and engage with mathematics through talks, competitions, and interactive activities among the student community. Exponentia’26 marks the second edition of Exponentia and is organized under the guidance of the Faculty Advisor, Dr. Jitraj Saha, AP\Maths.
